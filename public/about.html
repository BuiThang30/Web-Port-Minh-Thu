ABOUT ESG COMPASS

ESG COMPASS is a practical digital tool designed to help Vietnamese garment and apparel businesses understand their energy use, carbon impact, green-transition readiness, and potential investment opportunities.

The tool helps businesses measure and analyze carbon footprint from your activity data, evaluating green transition readiness and providing financial feedback.

Note: The current is just a prototype, which does not fully account for three scopes and have yet given financial feedback.
