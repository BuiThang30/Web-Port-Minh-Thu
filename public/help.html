FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What information do I need to start?

At minimum, you should provide basic company information and monthly electricity consumption. Additional information such as electricity cost, fuel consumption, and energy-management practices can improve the assessment.

What if I don't know my electricity consumption?

Check your electricity bills or electricity records. If reliable information is unavailable, the assessment may be limited.

Are the recommendations guaranteed to reduce emissions?

No. Recommendations are screening-level suggestions based on the information provided and relevant assumptions. Actual savings depend on the equipment, process, implementation, operating conditions, and project design.

What should I do if information is missing?

Provide the information you can verify and leave unavailable fields blank where permitted. The application will indicate which missing information limits the calculation or financial assessment.

Why can't I see a payback period?

A payback period requires both a reliable investment/CAPEX estimate and annual cost savings. If either is unavailable, ESG Compass will not calculate or display a payback period.

What does "Not Currently Finance Ready" mean?

It does not mean that your company cannot obtain financing. It means that the information currently available is insufficient for ESG Compass to establish a basic financial case for the proposed investment.
